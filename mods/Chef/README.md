## Getting it

- **"Chef" profession**: starts with a deep fryer, a food processor, two batteries, two cooking oil jugs (10 L each), an oil strainer, the chef's recipe book and some nutrifruit, fillerweed, standard raw meat, fat meat and Peat. Starting cash −200.
- **Other professions**: call Wild and buy the "Retired Chef" network upgrade (600 favor) for a deep fryer, a food processor, a jug of oil, a strainer and the recipe book.

## Four dishes

| Dish | Base price | Made in | Ingredients |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fries | 200 | deep fryer, overnight | 1 nutrifruit |
| Fried chicken | 200 | deep fryer, overnight | 1 piece of meat |
| Meat burger | 300 | food processor, "Burger" | all the meat, nutrifruit (bun) and fillerweed in the grid |
| Super combo | 900 | food processor, "Combo" | burger + fries + fried chicken |

"Meat" is anything the game counts as meat: raw meats, morsels, fat meat, meat scrap, Peat, fish and eel meat...

## Quality

Each dish scores 0-100 points in five grades: Inedible (under 30, −50%), Plain (30-54, base price), Tasty (55-74, +50%), Chef's (75-89, +100%), Michelin (90+, +200%).

| Dish | Meat calories | Nutrifruit calories | Oil purity | Ratio | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fried chicken | 35 (full at 1200 kcal) | — | 45 | — | 20 |
| Fries | — | 25 (full at 384 kcal) | 55 | — | 20 |
| Meat burger | 30 (all meat combined) | — | — | 50 | 20 |
| Super combo | lowest score of the three |||||

- **Oil purity**: full points at 98%+, none at 70% or less.
- **Ratio**: by calories, ideal meat 55% : nutrifruit 30% : fillerweed 15%; zero points once the summed deviation reaches 0.5.
- **Modules**: both machines have the game's standard module grid; +100% quality gives the full 20 points (Advanced Scientist custom modules work too).
- Hover over a dish to see its grade, score and per-item points.

## Cooking oil

The oil is tracked by this mod itself (the game's liquid containers would be priced and sold as water). It is a mix of four parts that move proportionally when poured:

- **Fresh oil**: purity = its share.
- **Large crumbs**: +12 ml per portion fried. Drop the **oil strainer** on the fryer or a jug; 1 durability per 5 ml (200 durability).
- **Fine residue**: +6 ml per portion. Drop the fryer or a jug on a **water pitcher with a filter**; 2 filter durability per ml (the game's rule). Large crumbs can be filtered this way too, at 10 per ml, which wears filters out fast.
- **Aged oil**: each night of frying turns 4% of the fresh oil into aged oil, which can't be filtered, so cleaned "recycled oil" never gets back to 100%; pouring in fresh oil helps.
- Each portion soaks up 100 ml; the fryer holds up to 5 L and needs at least 1 L. Below 70% purity it is spent oil; "Dump oil" in the fryer's title bar (two clicks within 3 s) empties it.

## Recipe book

A game paper book (`Books/chef_recipes.json`, generated by `tools/make_book.py`, same notebook as the game's appraisal guides) with five pages: dishes and grades, deep fryer, food processor, oil and filtering, ingredient calories. Open it like the game's own manuals.

## How to test

> Back up your saves first and test with a new game.

1. New game → pick "Chef" (chef's hat icon); storage holds the starting items.
2. Open the recipe book and flip through the five pages.
3. Drop an oil jug on the fryer ("Poured 5.0 L"); hover the fryer to see the oil.
4. Battery in the fryer, nutrifruit and fat meat in the basket, sleep: fries and fried chicken in the output; hover them for grade and score; the oil's purity has dropped.
5. Strainer on the fryer, then the fryer on a pitcher with a filter: the oil becomes recycled oil.
6. Food processor: "Burger" with meat, nutrifruit and fillerweed; "Combo" with a burger, fries and fried chicken.
7. Another profession: Wild's network has "Retired Chef" (600 favor).
8. Search `Player.log` for `[Chef]` and `[PaperUI]`.
