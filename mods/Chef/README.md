## Getting it

- **"Chef" profession**: starts with a deep fryer, a food processor, two batteries, two cooking oil jugs (10 L each), an oil strainer, the chef's recipe book and some nutrifruit, fillerweed, standard raw meat, fat meat and Peat. Starting cash −200.
- **Other professions**: call Wild and buy the "Retired Chef" network upgrade (600 favor) for a deep fryer, a food processor, a jug of oil, a strainer and the recipe book.

## Four dishes

| Dish | Base price | Made in | Ingredients |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fries | 200 | deep fryer, overnight | 1 nutrifruit |
| Fried chicken | 200 | deep fryer, overnight | 1 piece of meat |
| Meat burger | 300 | food processor, "Burger" | all the meat, nutrifruit (bun) and fillerweed in the grid |
| Super combo | 900 | food processor, "Combo" | burger + fries + fried chicken |

"Meat" is anything the game counts as meat: raw meats, morsels, fat meat, meat scrap, Peat, fish and eel meat...

## Quality

Each dish scores 0-100 points in five grades: Inedible (under 30, −50%), Plain (30-54, base price), Tasty (55-74, +50%), Chef's (75-89, +100%), Michelin (90+, +200%).

| Dish | Meat calories | Nutrifruit calories | Oil purity | Ratio | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fried chicken | 35 (full at 1200 kcal) | — | 45 | — | 20 |
| Fries | — | 25 (full at 384 kcal) | 55 | — | 20 |
| Meat burger | 30 (all meat combined) | — | — | 50 | 20 |
| Super combo | lowest score of the three |||||

- **Oil purity**: full points at 98%+, none at 70% or less.
- **Ratio**: by calories, ideal meat 55% : nutrifruit 30% : fillerweed 15%; zero points once the summed deviation reaches 0.5.
- **Modules**: both machines have the game's standard module grid; +100% quality gives the full 20 points (Advanced Scientist custom modules work too).
- Hover over a dish to see its grade, score and per-item points.

## Cooking oil

Cooking oil is a new liquid in the game's liquid system: it goes into any of the game's jugs and bottles and pours and drains like water. Four parts are added to the game's liquid table:

| Part | Particle size | Comes from | Removed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooking oil | 5 | the cooking oil jug holds 10 L | — (purity = its share) |
| Aged oil | 5 | each night of frying turns 4% of the oil into aged oil | can't be filtered |
| Large fry crumbs | 200 | +12 ml per portion | pour through a water pitcher with the **oil strainer** (filter size 150); a water filter works too but at 5 durability per ml |
| Fine oil residue | 40 | +6 ml per portion | pour through a water pitcher with a **water filter** (size 22), 2 durability per ml |

- Filtering is the game's own water pitcher (`WaterHelper.HandleFilter` removes parts by particle size and wears the filter). The strainer has 2000 durability, about 400 ml of crumbs.
- Used, filtered oil is "recycled oil": the aged oil stays, so it never gets back to 100%; fresh oil brings it up. Under 70% it is spent oil.
- **No water in the oil**, or the fryer won't fry.
- The game treats every liquid container as water. Containers that are mostly oil are patched: no water purity shown, value from the oil (12 per liter of cooking oil, 4 per liter of aged oil), household goods instead of water.

## Machines

- **Deep fryer** (3×2, 20 charge per night): power, modules, oil (a container with oil), basket (6×4), output (6×6). Each portion takes 100 ml from the container proportionally; it needs at least 1 L.
- **Food processor** (3×2, 5 charge per click): power, modules, ingredients (8×6), plating (7×4), output (6×6); "Burger" and "Combo" in the title bar.

## Recipe book

A game paper book (`Books/chef_recipes.json`, generated by `tools/make_book.py`) on the game's own notebook paper and buttons, six pages: dishes, deep fryer, food processor, oil and filtering, ingredient calories (table-of-contents style: Fat meat....1250), and quality (last page, colored grades). The game build lacks the paper sprite catalog, so the notebook and button sprites are looked up by name among the game's loaded sprites before opening (nothing is copied from the game).

## How to test

> Back up your saves first and **test with a new game** (0.1.0 oil jugs and fryers have the old structure).

1. New game → pick "Chef" (a round icon with a chef's hat, like the other professions).
2. Open the recipe book: the game's notebook, page buttons and close button; six pages.
3. Hover the oil jug: cooking oil 10 L, fresh oil, 100% purity, no water shown.
4. Fryer: oil jug in the oil slot, battery, nutrifruit and meat in the basket, sleep: fries and fried chicken; the oil's purity drops.
5. Pitcher with the strainer, pour the oil in (crumbs gone); swap in a water filter and pour again (residue gone): recycled oil.
6. Add a little water to the oil: the fryer says it can't fry.
7. Food processor: "Burger" and "Combo".
8. Another profession: Wild's network has "Retired Chef" (600 favor).
9. `Player.log`: "[Chef] 油的显示 / 定价补丁：4/4", "书用的游戏贴图已就位", no upgrade registration error.
