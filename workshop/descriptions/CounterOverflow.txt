[h1]【修复】掌柜帮手·柜台不丢货[/h1]
[b]修游戏 bug，会稍微影响平衡。[/b] 界面中英双语，跟随游戏语言。

[h2]修的是什么[/h2]
卖家（小偷、卖水的、杰克逊博士推销设备……）往柜台摆货时，原版里柜台放不下的货会被[b]直接销毁[/b]，没有任何提示；柜台全满时后面的货甚至不生成。杰克逊博士来一次就可能丢掉二十多件。

[h2]怎么修[/h2]
[list]
[*]放不下的货分到后面的[b]页[/b]里，一页正好摆满一个柜台。
[*]顾客头上的提示下方出现翻页条：上一页 / 第 1/3 页 / 下一页，可以直接点；也可以按 [b]Z[/b] 和 [b]X[/b]，按键可在游戏“选项”里改。
[*]每一页记住每件货的位置，翻走再翻回来原样摆回；你拿下来的货（比如拖到秤上议价）留下的空位不会被别的货占掉，随时能放回去。
[*]晚上去杰克逊博士的店里买东西时也一样分页，翻页条在柜台正上方。
[*]卖家给批发价时，批发连同所有页一起买下（价格已包含）。
[*]顾客离开时，没摆出来的页按原版处理柜台剩货的方式处理。
[*]议价、鉴定都在真货上进行，和原版一样。
[/list]

[h2]对平衡的影响[/h2]
你能看到原本会被销毁的货（例如黑市声望高时小偷的战利品箱），进货机会比原版多。

[h2]安装[/h2]
订阅后：主菜单 → 改装件 → 选中本 mod → 审阅并启用 → 重启游戏。不需要任何特殊权限。

[h1][Fix] Shopkeeper's Kit: Counter Overflow[/h1]
[b]Fixes a game bug, with a small effect on balance.[/b] UI in Chinese and English, following the game language.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AiChaosN/GAME_ProbablyStolen-ShopkeepersKit/main/mods/CounterOverflow/docs/images/how-it-works.png[/img]

[h2]The bug[/h2]
When a seller (thieves, water sellers, Dr. Jackson offering equipment, ...) lays goods on the counter, anything that doesn't fit is [b]silently destroyed[/b] in the base game; when the counter is completely full, the remaining items are never created. One visit from Dr. Jackson can lose twenty or more items.

[h2]The fix[/h2]
[list]
[*]Items that don't fit go onto extra [b]pages[/b]; one page is exactly one counter's worth.
[*]A page bar appears below the notification above the customer: Prev / Page 1/3 / Next, clickable; or press [b]Z[/b] and [b]X[/b]. Keys can be rebound in the game's Options.
[*]Each page remembers where every item sat and restores it exactly; the gap left by an item you took off (e.g. onto the scale to negotiate) stays free, so you can always put it back.
[*]Works at Dr. Jackson's shop at night too; the page bar sits right above the counter.
[*]A wholesale offer buys every page (the price already includes them).
[*]When the customer leaves, pages not on display are handled like leftover counter items in the base game.
[*]Negotiation and appraisal happen on the real items, as in the base game.
[/list]

[h2]Balance[/h2]
You will see goods the base game would have destroyed (e.g. thieves' loot crates at high Black Market reputation), so you get more buying opportunities.

[h2]Install[/h2]
After subscribing: main menu → Mods → select this mod → Review and enable → restart the game. No special capabilities required.
