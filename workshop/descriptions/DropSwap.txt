[h1]【便利】掌柜帮手·拖放换件[/h1]
[b]不改任何数值，只帮你省操作。[/b] 界面中英双语，跟随游戏语言。

[h2]做什么[/h2]
原版里换电池、换滤水器要先打开机器窗口，把旧件拖出来再把新件拖进去；直接拖到合着的机器上，槽里有旧件时只会标红。本 mod 让这一下直接生效：
[list]
[*][b]换电池[/b]：把新电池拖到机器上，换下里面的旧电池，旧电池放到新电池原来的位置。
[*][b]换滤水器[/b]：把新滤水器拖到水罐上，同理。
[*]悬停时显示绿色的使用提示，松手就换。
[/list]

[h2]不会改变的[/h2]
[list]
[*]槽空着时照原版直接放进去；电池拖到电池上互换等原版效果照旧。
[*]槽自己的规则照旧（例如水罐不收紫外线灯）。
[*]只对你自己的东西生效，一次换一件。
[*]电池、滤水器可以在改装件设置里分别关闭。
[/list]

[h2]安装[/h2]
订阅后：主菜单 → 改装件 → 选中本 mod → 审阅并启用 → 重启游戏。不需要任何特殊权限。

[h1][QoL] Shopkeeper's Kit: Drop Swap[/h1]
[b]No balance changes, it only saves clicks.[/b] UI in Chinese and English, following the game language.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
In the base game, swapping a battery or a filter means opening the machine, dragging the old part out and the new one in; dropping the new part on the closed machine only shows red while the old part is inside. This mod makes that drop work:
[list]
[*][b]Batteries[/b]: drop a new battery on a machine; it replaces the battery inside and the old one goes where the new one was.
[*][b]Filters[/b]: drop a new filter on a water pitcher, same thing.
[*]Hovering shows the green "use on" hint; release to swap.
[/list]

[h2]Unchanged[/h2]
[list]
[*]Empty slots are filled as in the base game; battery-on-battery swaps and other base-game drops work as before.
[*]Slot rules still apply (e.g. a water pitcher refuses UV filters).
[*]Only your own items, one at a time.
[*]Batteries and filters can be turned off separately in the mod settings.
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
After subscribing: main menu → Mods → select this mod → Review and enable → restart the game. No special capabilities required.
