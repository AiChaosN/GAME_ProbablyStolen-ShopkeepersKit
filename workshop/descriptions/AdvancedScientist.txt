[h1]【玩法】掌柜帮手·高级科学家[/h1]
[b]新职业，改变游戏机制。[/b] 界面中英双语，跟随游戏语言。词条合成机后续加入。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AiChaosN/GAME_ProbablyStolen-ShopkeepersKit/main/mods/AdvancedScientist/docs/images/machines.png[/img]

[h2]职业[/h2]
职业选择界面第 2 页的“高级科学家”，精通模组工艺。开局：机器区、空白模组制造机、词条镶嵌机、两块电池、三块随机模组、一份低级空白模组的材料。
[list]
[*][b]职业能力[/b]：直接放在机器区里的机器每天按两天运转（多耗一次电；冰箱、发电类等不加倍；设置里可关）。
[/list]

[h2]机器：点按钮立刻做[/h2]
打开机器窗口，标题栏 × 左边有“制造 / 提取 / 镶嵌”按钮，点一下做一次、从电源槽的电池扣 30 电；提示里写着做出了什么，或者为什么没做成（材料不够、产出格满了、没电……）。
[list]
[*][b]空白模组制造机[/b]：材料格放材料，点“制造”。材料够哪档做哪档，优先做高档：
[list]
[*]低级空白模组（可镶 2 个词条）= 普通电子元件 ×2 + 金属锭 ×2 + 线缆 ×2
[*]中级空白模组（可镶 4 个词条）= 普通电子元件 ×4 + 金属锭 ×4 + 线缆 ×4
[*]高级空白模组（可镶 6 个词条）= 普通电子元件 ×6 + 金属锭 ×6 + 线缆 ×6
[/list]
[*][b]词条提取机[/b]（找杰克逊博士买，只卖给高级科学家）：“模组”槽放一个模组，点“提取”：数值成一块基础芯片，每个特殊词条成一块特殊词条芯片，模组变回空白模组。神经核心等违禁品、赃物也能提取，出来的东西是干净的；功能型模组、节点、损坏的不收（放的时候会提示原因）。
[*][b]词条镶嵌机[/b]：“空白模组”槽放空白模组，“芯片”格放芯片，点“镶嵌”：基础芯片的数值相加（单块芯片每项上限 ±500），特殊词条按游戏规则最多 2 个、不能互斥。
[/list]

[h2]安装[/h2]
订阅后：主菜单 → 改装件 → 选中本 mod → 审阅并启用 → 重启游戏，新游戏时选这个职业。不需要任何特殊权限。

[h1][Gameplay] Shopkeeper's Kit: Advanced Scientist[/h1]
[b]A new profession that changes game mechanics.[/b] UI in Chinese and English, following the game language. A chip merger comes later.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AiChaosN/GAME_ProbablyStolen-ShopkeepersKit/main/mods/AdvancedScientist/docs/images/machines.png[/img]

[h2]Profession[/h2]
"Advanced Scientist" on page 2 of the profession screen, a module-crafting expert. Starts with a Machine Bay, the Blank Module Maker, the Affix Inlayer, two batteries, three random modules and materials for one low-grade blank module.
[list]
[*][b]Ability[/b]: machines placed directly in a Machine Bay run two days' worth every day (using power again; fridges, generators etc. excluded; can be turned off).
[/list]

[h2]Machines: click a button, done[/h2]
Open a machine and click "Make / Extract / Inlay" left of × in its title bar. Each click runs once and takes 30 charge from the battery in the power slot; a message tells you what was made or why not (not enough materials, output full, no power, ...).
[list]
[*][b]Blank Module Maker[/b]: put materials in the grid and click "Make". It makes the best grade the materials allow:
[list]
[*]Low-grade blank module (2 slots) = Common Electronic Parts ×2 + Metal Ingot ×2 + Wire ×2
[*]Mid-grade blank module (4 slots) = Common Electronic Parts ×4 + Metal Ingot ×4 + Wire ×4
[*]High-grade blank module (6 slots) = Common Electronic Parts ×6 + Metal Ingot ×6 + Wire ×6
[/list]
[*][b]Affix Extractor[/b] (sold by Dr. Jackson, to Advanced Scientists only): put a module in the "Module" slot and click "Extract": its stats become a base chip and each special affix an affix chip; the module turns back into a blank. Contraband such as neural cores and stolen goods work too, and the results are clean; functional modules, nodes and corrupt modules are refused (with a message saying why).
[*][b]Affix Inlayer[/b]: put a blank module in the "Blank module" slot and chips in the "Chips" grid, then click "Inlay": base chip stats add up (each chip caps at ±500 per stat); special affixes follow the game's rules (at most 2, no conflicting ones).
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
After subscribing: main menu → Mods → select this mod → Review and enable → restart, then pick the profession in a new game. No special capabilities required.
