[h2]收货：一键归箱[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]G[/b]：仓库里散放的货自动归进对应的箱子（储藏区、工具箱、背包）。
[*]每件货属于 13 个大类之一：食物、饮料与水、日用品、医疗、化学、武器与弹药、材料、工具与设备、模组、种子与农业、养殖、文件证件、奢侈品与收藏。
[*]箱子收什么：点箱子窗口标题栏 × 左边的 [b]分类[/b] 按钮（或指着箱子按 Ctrl+G），勾整类或单件货。不勾 = 看箱子里现有的东西（有同款的优先）。分类面板只列出你在这个存档里见过的货，不剧透。
[*]升级过的储藏区（扩容套件，多栏）：每栏单独设置、单独归箱；一栏放满了，接着放进同一个箱子里空着的栏。
[*]箱子（或某一栏）可以设成[b]不参与归箱[/b]：自用的药品区、装备区不会再被塞进新货。
[*]箱子右下角有角标（约占箱子的一半），不用打开就知道柜子里装的是什么。
[*]指着柜台按 G：柜台上你的货放回箱子或仓库。
[*]Shift+G：买下顾客的货时自动归箱（默认关）。
[/list]

[h2]卖货：一键上柜台[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]C[/b]：把当前买家会买的货全部摆上柜台（用的是游戏自己判断“顾客买不买”的规则）。
[*]指着仓库或箱子里的某件货按 C：把它的同款全部摆上柜台；指着柜台上的货按 C：反过来把同款收回箱子。
[*]可选：从仓库或箱子拿一件放上柜台，同款自动跟上（默认关，Shift+C 打开）。
[*][b]同款[/b]按物品提示里看得到的标签区分：类型、水质 / 药品纯度等等级（没检测过按表面等级，不替你偷看）、瓶子里装的液体。装酒精的瓶子不会跟着水瓶上柜台。
[*]默认只摆顾客买得起的；你自己调配的混合液（瓶子里有水以外的液体）不会被摆出去。
[*]箱子可以设成[b]不参与上柜台[/b]：你自己要用的设备、不想卖的东西放进去，C 永远不会动它们。
[/list]

钉住的物品、机器、箱子本身不会被搬；鉴定指南、扫描仪这类装备不会被摆上柜台。所有热键都可以在游戏“选项”里按键绑定。

[h2]安装[/h2]
订阅后：主菜单 → 改装件 → 选中本 mod → 审阅并启用 → 重启游戏。不需要任何特殊权限。

[h1][QoL] Shopkeeper's Kit: Sort To Boxes[/h1]
[b]No stat changes, no extra information; it only saves you clicks.[/b] UI in Chinese and English, following the game language.

[h2]Storing: one-key sorting[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]G[/b]: loose items in storage go into the right box (storage bays, toolbox, backpacks).
[*]Every item belongs to one of 13 categories: food, drinks & water, household, medical, chemistry, weapons & ammo, materials, tools & equipment, modules, seeds & farming, husbandry, documents & cards, luxury & collectibles.
[*]What a box takes: click the [b]Sort[/b] button left of × in the box window's title bar (or Ctrl+G while pointing at the box) and tick whole categories or single items. Nothing ticked = decide by what the box already holds (identical items first). The panel only lists goods you have seen in this save, so nothing is spoiled.
[*]Upgraded storage bays (expansion kit, several tabs): each tab has its own settings and is sorted on its own; when a tab is full, items continue into an empty tab of the same box.
[*]A box (or a tab) can be set to [b]keep out of sorting[/b]: your own medicine or gear stash never gets new goods stuffed into it.
[*]A badge in each box's bottom-right corner (about half the box) shows what it holds.
[*]G while pointing at the counter: put your items there back into boxes or storage.
[*]Shift+G: auto-sort goods you buy from customers (off by default).
[/list]

[h2]Selling: one-key counter[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]C[/b]: put everything the current buyer would buy onto the counter (using the game's own "would this customer buy it" rule).
[*]C while pointing at an item in storage or a box: put all identical items on the counter; on an item on the counter: take them back into boxes.
[*]Optional: move one item from storage or a box onto the counter and its identical copies follow (off by default; Shift+C turns it on).
[*][b]Identical[/b] means matching everything you can see in the tooltip: types, grades such as water quality and drug purity (the apparent grade if untested, so nothing hidden is revealed), and the liquids in a bottle. A bottle of alcohol won't follow a bottle of water onto the counter.
[*]By default only items the buyer can afford are put up, and never your own mixtures (bottles holding liquids other than water).
[*]Boxes can be marked [b]keep out of the counter[/b]: put your own equipment and things you won't sell there and C never touches them.
[/list]

Pinned items, machines and boxes themselves are never moved; guides, scanners and similar equipment are never put up for sale. Every hotkey can be bound in the game's Options.
